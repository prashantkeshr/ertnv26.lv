# Content policy

## Independence

eport is an independent platform. It never impersonates or implies affiliation with a government, customs department, port authority, international organization, embassy or regulator. No government seals, fabricated certification marks, invented endorsements or government-style branding. Directory pages state that the summary is eport's own and that the body has not endorsed it.

## Official vs. editorial

- Summaries are written by eport and labelled "eport editorial summary". Official text is not copied.
- Official forms are never reproduced; eport links to the issuing authority's own portal.
- Commercial templates (Phase 3) are labelled preparatory/commercial and are never presented as official certificates.

## Guidance, not advice

Output is preliminary guidance — never a legal determination, official clearance, customs assessment or financial advice. Readiness scores and risk assessments (Phase 3) must say so and show what was assumed, what is missing and what is unknown.

## Copyright

No scraping or reproduction of unlicensed articles. News records (Phase 5) carry headline, short original summary, publisher, date and a link to the original. Incoterms® and other ICC content are referenced with attribution; their text is not reproduced without licence. HS nomenclature text is not published until its licensing is resolved.

## Businesses and professionals

No invented phone numbers, e-mail addresses, office addresses or credentials. "Verified" is never used because a site or e-mail exists: statuses are official website located / public information available / source-referenced / identity independently verified / verification pending / not verified.

## Events and opportunities

No invented events, deadlines or tenders; each needs organizer, country, date, official link, publication date and verification status.

## Editorial review

New regulatory content is drafted against the official source with the fields in DATA_POLICY.md, passes schema validation and link checks, and is marked `officially-verified` only after an editor's comparison. Stale links flip a record to `needs-review` automatically.

## Scope honesty

If a feature cannot work without a backend it is marked Coming Soon, specifically. If data is partial, the page says so and links to the official source. A landing page does not make a phase "complete".

## Logos, flags and marks

- **Country flags** (flag-icons, MIT) are used to identify nations and national authorities.
- **Organization logos are not reproduced.** WTO, IMO, ISO, ICC and similar marks are protected, and showing them beside "references only" could still suggest a partnership or endorsement. International bodies appear as neutral typographic monograms. A licensed or permissioned logo can be added later with the organization's written approval.
- The independence statement ("not a government, customs, port or regulatory service") appears in the footer, About page, contact section and FAQ rather than in a persistent top banner.

## News

Headline, publisher abstract, date and link only. Topic tags are automatic and labelled as such. Live web headlines are unverified and shown apart from official-publisher news.

## Videos

Only videos uploaded by the organization itself, or by its own academy or national committee, are listed, and the real channel name is shown on every card. Third-party explainers are excluded even when they are accurate. Each video is checked against YouTube oEmbed (exists, embeddable, same uploader and title). Videos are general explainers, never presented as guidance for a particular country or shipment, and are not an endorsement of eport by their publishers.

## Country guides

A country guide is an eport editorial overview of how exporting and importing is generally organised, with each stage linked to the responsible authority. It is marked **Needs review** until an editor has compared it line by line with the official sources, states its limits beside the steps, and is never described as a list of requirements. Guides are published one market at a time; countries without one say so plainly. Nothing in a guide is presented as an official form, fee, tariff or deadline.

## What eport does not claim

Pages do not state shipping lane availability, carrier recommendations, lead times, restricted-item lists or service levels, because eport is an information platform and does not operate or quote freight, importer-of-record or clearance services. Where a similar slot exists on a page (for example restricted items) it either carries sourced information or says that none is published.
